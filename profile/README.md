## The rate they don't publish.

Tell us where you want to stay. Our agent contacts the hotel directly, asks what they can do on price, and
comes back with a quote you can hold for 24 hours. Free for you. Cheaper for them.

## How it works

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pick a hotel.** | Paste a link or search. Works with the hotel you already found somewhere else. |
| **2. We ask on your behalf.** | Our agent reaches the reservations desk, says it is an AI, and asks for availability and their best unpublished rate. |
| **3. You get a real quote.** | A named price, held for 24 hours. Nothing is charged until you accept. |

**The discount was always there.** Booking platforms charge hotels 15 to 25 percent on every reservation.
We charge five. That gap is your discount.
